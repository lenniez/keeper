A Tuesday with Keeper.

Meet Maya, an independent brand strategist with five clients and one very full phone.

7:48 AM · still on coffee number one
  1. 7:48 AM
    Her list is already waiting

    Overnight, Keeper caught three requests: two emails and a Slack message. Maya reads them before her coffee cools.

  2. 9:15 AM
    “I’ll send the revised timeline by Friday”

    Said on a Zoom call with Raj. It’s on her list, due Friday, before she even hangs up.

  3. 11:30 AM
    The scheduling back-and-forth that didn’t happen

    Tomi texts asking when she can see the new office. Keeper finds Wednesday at 2 and books it. Maya hears about it from her own calendar.

  4. 2:40 PM
    A thought in the parking lot

    “Order sample prints for Dana.” She says it once into her phone. Clean task, right client, done.

  5. 5:30 PM
    Five minutes of approvals

    Three drafted replies are ready. She sends two, tweaks one, and calls it a day.

  6. 6:00 PM
    Laptop closed

    Nothing slipped. Nobody’s waiting on her. Dinner is, though.
