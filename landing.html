Privacy

Your client work is yours to keep.

Keeper reads your conversations so you don’t have to. That takes trust, so we built it to earn it.

Never used to train AI

Your messages, files and client details are never used to train our models or anyone else’s. Our AI providers are contractually barred from it too.

Encrypted and private

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only you can see your board. No one at Keeper reads your messages.

Reads only what it needs

Keeper scans for commitments and keeps just the snippet that created each task. It never sends, deletes or edits anything without your permission.

Leave anytime, take nothing with us

Disconnect a channel and Keeper stops reading instantly. Delete your account and everything goes with it.
